KNOW THE BASICS

Stay on. Send them off.

Deal damage to increase knockback, then launch opponents beyond the arena. Each fighter has three stocks. Last fighter standing wins. At four minutes, stocks decide the winner, then lowest damage.

A / D Move left / right

W / S Aim strikes up / down

SPACE Jump · press again for an air jump

S Drop through thin platforms

SHIFT Dash / dodge with brief invulnerability

Left click Light strike · hold to keep attacking

Right click Heavy strike · hold and release to charge

E Pick up a glowing weapon near you

Every character can use all fifteen weapons. W + heavy aims up; S + heavy aims down. Ground heavies have character-specific opener, follow-up and confirmed finisher animations. Aerial heavies are shared per weapon. In the air, dodge with SHIFT and begin a heavy within a quarter-second to create a ghost platform and use the ground signature. Everyone starts unarmed. Press E near an identical mystery pickup to receive a random weapon; it is revealed only after collection. Light attacks are shared for a weapon; heavy techniques change with your fighter, weapon and direction.
