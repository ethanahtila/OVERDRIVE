# OVERDRIVE 1.0.0 artwork

Game artwork was made for this project using the built-in image-generation tool. The project bundles optimized WebP copies, so it does not contact an image service at runtime.

- `public/assets/worlds.webp`: six realistic fantasy environment paintings in a two-column, three-row atlas. The five regions use the first five panels; an alpine variant uses the sixth. The code crops and shades the paintings while drawing actual platforms separately.
- `public/assets/fighters.webp`: five original transparent full-body character cutouts. They are used in selection portraits and procedurally deformed in gameplay. Weapon art and effects are drawn separately in code.

## Environment prompt

“Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single production game-background texture atlas for OVERDRIVE, a smooth realistic 2D side-view platform fighting game. Make a seamless edge-to-edge contact sheet, EXACTLY 2 columns by 3 rows of equally sized landscape panels, no borders, no gutters, no text. Each panel is a complete cinematic environment background, 16:9, side-on view toward the distance, horizon about 58% from top, foreground empty atmospheric space reserved for gameplay platforms drawn later; NO floating platforms and NO people. Top left: realistic misty alpine valley with distant intricate ancient cliffside castles, grey teal mountains, honey sunlight through clouds. Top right: storm-cleared coastal lagoon, weathered lighthouse on distant rocky headland, turquoise sea, gold reflections. Middle left: ancient lush forest sanctuary, majestic natural gnarled trees, detailed moss and leaf canopy, fog and volumetric sunbeams. Middle right: vast ancient moonlit ruined cathedral, distant spires, lavender moon, carved stone architecture, subtle magical atmosphere. Bottom left: obsidian volcanic caldera with distant ruined fortress, lava glow below, hot ash against a smoky plum sky. Bottom right: dramatic windswept alpine ruins at blue hour with waterfall and scattered golden lights. Style: high-fidelity realistic matte-painted scenery, fine material detail, natural atmospheric perspective, believable mountains and stone, beautifully art-directed lighting. A polished serious fantasy fighting game, never cartoon, never pixel art, never low-poly, no square blocks, no UI. Avoid foreground structures that might be mistaken for collision geometry. Overall canvas ideally 2048 by 1728.”

## Fighter prompt

“Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: five-character sprite and portrait atlas for the original 2D fighting game OVERDRIVE. A single transparent PNG canvas, exactly FIVE equal-width vertical columns in one row. Each column contains one complete full-body fighter, centered in their own column with generous transparent padding, all same height, feet aligned along baseline near bottom, heads near top. No overlapping between columns, no text, no ground shadows, no props, NO weapons. All fighters face screen RIGHT in a clean three-quarter side view, readable guarded neutral combat stance, both hands empty near waist in front of body, legs slightly separated. Realistic anatomical proportions with refined illustrative fantasy realism, detailed cloth, worn leather, brushed metal, realistic human faces, dramatic soft rim lighting; smooth edges, no pixels, no blocks, no chibi, no cartoons. The five ORIGINAL characters left to right: 1 EMBER a lean athletic male fire knight, dark reddish leather under fitted charcoal steel plate armor with subtle ember-orange inlays, warm brown skin, short dark hair, tiny orange plume, no helmet covering face. 2 LYRA an athletic female wind ranger with light silver hair tied back, pale teal flowing scarf, fitted deep blue and teal leather tunic, light silvery shoulder armor, agile silhouette. 3 BASTION a broad heavy male armored guardian with weathered pale gold and grey steel plate, dramatic layered pauldrons, dark beard, bare face, most massive silhouette. 4 NYX a lean androgynous dusk assassin with muted violet hood, tailored charcoal and lavender layered leather, lower face covered, very distinct thin silhouette. 5 MARIN a male tide monk with dark wavy hair, olive skin, sea green layered fabric, ivory sash crossing the torso, leather bracers, fitted trousers. Feet wear boots that remain fully visible. Keep cloth close enough to body for separate columns. Atlas ideally 2560 by 1024. Genuinely transparent background.”
